### [RESOLVED] Roofs now use Epic's own roof materials and carry rooftop equipment
Follow-up to the roof-slab entry below. `roofs.py` now gives each building one of four migrated Epic roof materials (`MI_Rooftop_BitumenRoofing`, `DirtyConcreteTiles`, `PebbleDash`, `RoofTile`, tags `roof_0`..`roof_3`, chosen by `building_id % 4`; Epic picks one of six by `Building_ID % 6`, two are not migrated) instead of the borrowed asphalt, and `generate_roof_prop_pieces` scatters real rooftop equipment on each slab (`Kit_roof_AC_Unit_B`, `Kit_Roof_Vent_RR`, `Kit_roof_exhaust_RR`, sizes measured in the engine). The prop LAYOUT is INFERRED, not measured (Epic places whole prop Blueprints from a 1.2GB kit whose internals were not read): about one item per 60m2 of roof (max six), 3m apart, 1.5m from the edge, random 90-degree yaw, deterministic per building. Verified live in a real generated city from above: varied grey/tan roofs with visible AC units and vents. Rooftop props are only emitted when the scene is dressed (with an environment), like the ground and roofs.
