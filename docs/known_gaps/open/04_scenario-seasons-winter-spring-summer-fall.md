### [RESOLVED, no foliage] Scenario seasons: winter, spring, summer, fall
`environment.py` gained `Season` and per-season environment presets (`season_environment`), and `generate_scenario(..., season=None)` records `ScenarioResult.season` (fixed by the caller, or drawn uniformly from the scenario seed as the LAST draw of the style stream so every earlier style choice for a seed is unchanged). A season sets the sun angle and colour temperature, fog, exposure, saturation and an optional colour gain (`EnvironmentConfig` gained `sun_temperature_k` and `color_gain`, both already understood by the plugin, so no C++ change) and decides whether street trees appear: only winter and fall keep them, because Epic's tree kits are bare branch skeletons (see the tree entries below), so leafy seasons simply have no street trees rather than dead-looking ones. `bin/send_scenario_to_ue5.py` sends the scenario's own season preset. The preset values are tuned by eye against this scene, NOT measured: summer high bright sun (-58 deg pitch), spring softer hazier light (-45, 6800K), fall low warm sun (-24, 4600K, warm gain), winter very low cool sun (-16, 8500K, cool gain, saturation 0.6, heavier fog). Verified live by rendering one real city in each season (summer bright and tree-free, fall warm gold with bare trees, winter cool and muted with bare trees). Not done: snow or wet ground, leaf colour, seasonal sky/cloud changes, a time-of-day axis.



**Update 2026-10-09:** with `road_network.foliage: true` (template `urban_dense_v16.yaml`) spring, summer and fall now have generated leafy trees with a season tint; see docs/experiments/32_*.md. Winter keeps the bare trees; snow and wet ground are still not done.
