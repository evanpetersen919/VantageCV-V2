## What the numbers say

- **Riders are rare in images and small in the frame.** Only 5.1% of images contain a rider, 6.2% a bike and 3.3% a
  motor. The median box is about 52 to 59 px tall in a 720 px image, and the 5th percentile is 15 to 19 px. At the
  960 px training size used by the project's detector, those medians are about 40 to 44 px.
- **The validation set is small for these classes:** 649 riders, 1,007 bikes and 452 motors. That limits how small an
  AP change can be detected (see the pre-registration).
- **Most bike and motor boxes have no rider on them.** Depending on the overlap threshold, 58 to 72% of bike and motor
  boxes have no overlapping rider box (65.2% of bikes and 63.7% of motors at 0.3). They are parked or unattended
  vehicles, or vehicles whose rider is not separately labelled. A generator that only produces ridden bikes would
  produce the wrong mix: unridden vehicles are the majority.
- **Pairing depends on the threshold.** 87.4%, 79.6% or 66.0% of riders overlap a bike or motor box (thresholds 0.1,
  0.3, 0.5). The remaining riders have no overlapping vehicle box, which can mean a rider on another kind of vehicle
  (scooter, skateboard) or an unlabelled vehicle.
- **Two riders on one vehicle exist but are rare:** 56 bikes and 23 motors at threshold 0.3.
- **Day and night differ a lot:** a rider appears in 7.1% of daytime images but 2.65% of night images, so night rider
  images are the scarcest case. Median box height is larger at night for riders (68 px, against 57 by day) and
  bikes (61 against 50); motors are close (53 against 50).
- **The `occluded` flag is nearly always true** for riders (89%) and bikes (84%) and true for 77% of motors, so it
  cannot be matched with a visibility fraction without first defining what the flag means. How BDD's annotators set
  it is not documented in the files measured here. Open question: calibrate it against this project's own
  `visibility_fraction` on cars, whose flag rate is 67.7%.
- **139 train images have an undefined time of day** and are only in the "all" rows.

## What this does not contain

Lean angle, rider speed, lane position, clothing and helmet use, and sidewalk versus road position are not in the
labels; no sourced value for them was found, so the generator does not claim to match them. Box geometry, counts,
pairing, occlusion flags and time-of-day shares above are the measurable targets.
